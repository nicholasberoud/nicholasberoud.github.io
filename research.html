Research

  • Published · Independent research · Updated 13 November 2026

    Fiscal Deficits and the Treasury Term Premium: A Weaker Association After 2008 and the Investor-Base Hypothesis

    Did the US Treasury term premium stop responding to deficits after 2008? Split-sample comparisons generally find a weaker association after 2008; how much weaker depends on how the premium’s baseline is modelled. The second half asks whether a changing investor base could help explain it, using annual foreign-holdings data that describe ownership but cannot by themselves show cause or price sensitivity.

    My part: the questions and interpretation; code and checks AI-assisted under my direction · Pricing analysis published; ownership interpretation under review · Data and downloads

  • In progress · Research assistant work · Started November 2026

    AI Infrastructure and Neocloud Financing

    How much have specialised GPU-cloud companies borrowed, how has their leverage changed, and what assets has that borrowing financed or secured? The work starts with CoreWeave’s filings and financing agreements; four other companies follow.

    With Professor Pedro Gete · First CoreWeave debt history checked against the filings · No analysis or findings yet

  • Working draft · Treasury follow-up · November 2026

    Selling or Repricing? Foreign Holdings of Long-Term Treasuries

    When foreign holdings of long-term Treasuries fall in a month, how much reflects selling and how much reflects moving prices? It reproduces the decomposition the Federal Reserve publishes for official and private holders.

    Unfinished: external checks outstanding and the interpretation is being rewritten

  • Proposal stage · Undergraduate thesis · IE University

    Legislative Constraints and Sovereign Risk: Domestic Institutions in Latin American Debt Markets

    Are stronger legislative constraints on the executive associated with lower dollar sovereign spreads in Latin America, after accounting for fiscal and economic conditions?

    Proposal submitted November 2026; awaiting my advisor’s feedback · No findings

  • Market note · 29 October 2026 · Data to 28 October

    Brazil Prices Lula: Anatomy of an Eight-Sigma Day

    What did Brazil’s equity, currency and sovereign-bond repricing after the election tell us? It examines how unusual the equity move looks under different distributional assumptions, and why the assets need not tell the same story.

    Dated commentary on one event, not a forecast
