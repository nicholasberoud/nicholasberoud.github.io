Nicholas Beroud

Exploring sovereign lending markets, the economics of AI, and political economy.

I am a Master of Science in Finance student at IE New York College and write about sovereign lending markets, how politics gets priced in government debt and, now, the economics of AI. Previously, I was a Senior Macro Research Analyst at IE University’s EDEN Fund and a Political Section intern at the U.S. Embassy in Madrid. I also advise a collaborative Treasury repo-market model and helped build a supply-disruption loss model for NVIDIA’s tin supply chain at the NYU Ethical Tech CoLab Climate Intelligence Hackathon, hosted by Microsoft. I am completing a Bachelor in International Relations at IE University, with a thesis on legislative constraints and sovereign risk in Latin America, and I have passed CFA Level I.
