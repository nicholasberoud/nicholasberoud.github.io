AI Infrastructure and Neocloud FinancingBorrowing, Leverage and the Assets Behind the AI Build-Out

How much have specialised GPU-cloud companies borrowed, and what assets does that debt finance or secure?

The first case study is CoreWeave. Between December 2023 and September 2026 its debt (borrowings plus finance leases) rose from $1.5bn to $47.5bn, and from 30.7% to 51.9% of total assets, while its gross property and equipment grew from $3.6bn to $64.6bn. The filings say more clearly what lenders hold as security than what each loan paid for. The clearest link is equipment financing from manufacturers, secured by the same equipment it paid for.
