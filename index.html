I’m completing IE’s 3+1 program, combining a bachelor’s in International Relations at IE University with a Master in Finance at IE New York College. I’m part of the first MiF cohort in New York, where I also lead the Finance & Strategy Club.

Political economy drew me toward financial markets. I’m interested in fixed-income markets, particularly sovereign debt. My thesis examines how Federal Reserve institutional design and changes in who holds US government debt may shape the bond market’s response to fiscal deficits.

I also attend hackathons and collaborate on ideas for using AI to solve practical problems. The connection for me is understanding how policy and institutions affect markets, then using research and technology to investigate those questions.
