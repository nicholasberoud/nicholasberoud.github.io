Repo Market Model

Research collaboration · Updated 12 October 2026

What can public data tell us about pressure in overnight Treasury funding markets?

Repo is the market where cash is borrowed against securities, including US Treasuries. This project studies the conditions associated with pressure in that market and how a forecast can be evaluated using only information available at the time.

The collaboration

Eleonora Björnberg is the project’s author and owns its model, research design, engineering and evaluation decisions. I contribute as an advisor on data selection and economic interpretation: which public series represent the market’s mechanics, what those quantities mean, and which questions need economic judgment.

That role is documented in the project’s contributor statement.

What I Contribute

My work focuses on the economics behind the inputs: how bank reserves, the Treasury’s cash balance, money-market funds and settlement dates affect the availability of cash. I help connect those quantities to the market behaviour the model is trying to explain.

Model results, evaluation and limitations are maintained in Eleonora’s original project, linked below.
